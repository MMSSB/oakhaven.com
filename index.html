Oakhaven Vanguard:
The Sunstone Quest

Chronicles of the Royal Vanguard

The Lore

For a thousand years, the Sacred Sunstone blessed the kingdom of Oakhaven with eternal warmth. But from the eastern volcanic wastes, Overlord Malakor, Consumer of Suns, rose to shatter the peace.


Under cover of an eclipse, the Bloodfang Orc Horde seized the Sunstone, dragging it into eternal shadow. You are Captain Kael, the last Knight of the Royal Vanguard. Take up your Sunforged Greatsword and march east through perilous regions to reclaim the light!

Game Modes

Story Campaign

An epic 8-Zone narrative journey spanning from the Whispering Woods to the Sunstone Sanctum.

  • Handcrafted multi-layered terrain
  • Sacred Checkpoints & Lore Shrines
  • Mid-Boss duel: Warlord Grommash
  • Final Showdown: Overlord Malakor

Endless Vanguard Run

A non-stop, procedurally generated action runner where the world builds infinitely eastward.

  • Dynamic difficulty waves
  • Bonus valleys & platform gauntlets
  • Battle Colossal Giant Orc Titans
  • Live score multiplier & global ranks

Arsenal & Abilities

Weapons

  • Sunforged Greatsword: Fast 2-hit combos and heavy cleaves for armor breaking.
  • Eagle-Eye Longbow: Ranged precision. Upgrade to unlock twin arrows and triple volleys.
  • Alchemist's Belt: Manage Crimson, Emerald, and Golden potions on the fly.

Vanguard Skills

  • Perfect Parry: Time your Shield Guard to reflect damage and stun attackers.
  • Sky Plunge Slam: Dive rapidly from mid-air to unleash a ground shockwave.
  • Sunbreaker Ultimate: Unleash a massive golden AoE shockwave and heal your wounds!
